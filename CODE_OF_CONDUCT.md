# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, the Tyllage team members who build and maintain this project, want everyone who takes part to have a respectful, harassment-free experience. That holds whatever your age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with teammates who are learning.
- Give and accept constructive feedback on code, not on people.
- Credit others' work and ideas.
- Respect client confidentiality: MEOD's business information, data and unreleased plans stay within the project.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and fix them openly.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission, including customer data
- Trolling, personal or political attacks
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional or school setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, chats, meetings, and client sessions with MEOD. It also applies when you represent the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the project lead or the course supervisor. All reports will be handled confidentially and fairly. Responses can include a private warning, a temporary removal from project spaces, or escalation to the institution.
