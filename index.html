Our promise

Safe, fresh and sustainable

Safe & Healthy

Pesticide-free, chemical-free and GMO-free produce since 2014. Keeping our produce safe means keeping you safe.

Fresh & Nutritious

Daily harvests bring you fresh nutrients packed in the freshest, crispiest leaves.

Clean & Green

Our greenhouses conserve energy, and we recycle, reuse and upcycle to cut our carbon footprint.

Growing with passion

6 hectares to grow for you

Our Mobile Gulley System grows vegetables without soil. A Nutrient Film Technique stream maximises yield through automation and precise input control at every stage of the plant's life.

Out in the fruit crops, our natural allies — ladybugs — keep pests in check. Technology and nature, working together.
